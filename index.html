About Me

Stephen Kiraly, PT, DPT

Hey there! My name is Stephen and I am a Doctor of Physical Therapy with a passion to get my patients back to doing what they love. Whether that is climbing, running, hiking, or walking around the block, I'm here for you!

Outside of Pulse Physical Therapy, I currently work as an outpatient physical therapist, treating individuals of all ages with a wide range of injuries and conditions. I also have experience working in a neurological rehabilitation hospital, as well as at a VA hospital. In my spare time, I collaborate with The Climbing Doctor, helping produce rehabilitation and injury-prevention content for the @theclimbingdoctor Instagram community. I also teach in a climbing Special Interest Group (SIG), where we help current DPT students learn how to work with climbing athletes.

Outside of physical therapy, I am an avid trail runner, climber, soccer player, cyclist, snowboarder, traveler, backpacker, and enjoy all things outdoors.

FAQ

Physical therapy should be tailored to your individual needs, goals, and lifestyle. At Pulse Physical Therapy, I work with people of all ages and activity levels—from those experiencing difficulty with everyday movements to recreational and competitive athletes. Whether you want to walk without pain, return to work, get back on the climbing wall, or run your next race, your treatment will be designed around the activities that matter most to you. Together, we’ll use evidence-based care to help you move with greater confidence and return to doing what you love.

Pulse Physical Therapy is an out-of-network provider and does not accept insurance. Insurance requirements can sometimes affect visit structure, covered services, and treatment frequency. By operating outside those restrictions, care can be individualized around what you need during each session. This focused approach may also reduce the number of visits needed for some clients. We accept credit cards, debit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. All payments are due at the time of service.

Clients can request an itemized receipt, or superbill, to submit to their insurance company if they have out-of-network benefits. Reimbursement is determined by the client’s insurance plan and is not guaranteed.

We start with a deep dive into your injury history and specific athletic goals. From there, we perform a comprehensive movement assessment and sport-specific strength testing. By the end of the hour, you’ll have a clear understanding of your issue. You’ll leave with a few exercises to work on at home, as well as a plan of care that estimates how many treatment sessions we’ll likely need to get you back out there!

Follow-up sessions focus on dialing in your loading protocols, assessing progress through performance metrics, and adjusting your plan as you return to higher-intensity activities. These are active sessions designed to ensure long-term resilience, not just a quick fix.

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. No-shows and appointments canceled less than 24 hours before the scheduled start time will be charged the full session fee, except in cases of sudden illness or emergency.
