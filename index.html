About Me

Stephen Kiraly, PT, DPT

Hey there! My name is Stephen and I am a Doctor of Physical Therapy with a passion to get my patients back to doing what they love. Whether that is climbing, running, hiking, or walking around the block, I'm here for you!

I am a collaborator with The Climbing Doctor, where I help produce rehab and prehab content for the @theclimbingdoctor Instagram community. My work includes a featured article on hypermobility for the Climbing Doctor website and a guest appearance on his podcast, discussing specialized strategies for climbers.

Outside of physical therapy, I am an avid trail runner, climber, soccer player, cyclist, snowboarder, traveler, backpacker, and enjoy all things outdoors.

FAQ

Standard physical therapy often lacks the sport-specific knowledge required for high-level athletes. At Pulse Physical Therapy, we speak the language of your sport and understand what you need. Whether you are navigating a pulley strain on a boulder or shin splints on a trail, we use evidence-based loading protocols designed specifically for the demands of your sport.

No. Washington allows direct access to physical therapy for neuromuscular and musculoskeletal conditions, which means you can generally begin treatment without a referral. If you plan to seek out-of-network reimbursement, check with your insurance provider because your individual plan may still require a referral. If your symptoms indicate that another healthcare provider should be involved, I will help direct you to the appropriate care.

Pulse Physical Therapy is an out-of-network provider and does not accept insurance. Insurance requirements can sometimes affect visit structure, covered services, and treatment frequency. By operating outside those restrictions, care can be individualized around what you need during each session. This focused approach may also reduce the number of visits needed for some clients. We accept credit cards, debit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. All payments are due at the time of service.

Clients can request an itemized receipt, or superbill, to submit to their insurance company if they have out-of-network benefits. Reimbursement is determined by the client’s insurance plan and is not guaranteed.

We start with a deep dive into your injury history and specific athletic goals. From there, we perform a comprehensive movement assessment and sport-specific strength testing. By the end of the hour, you’ll have a clear understanding of your issue. Follow-up sessions focus dialling in your loading protocols, assessing progress through performance metrics, and adjusting your plan as you return to higher-intensity activities. These are active sessions designed to ensure long-term resilience, not just a quick fix.
